# 🌱 Morrow — The Little Internet Gardener

> *“Not every little idea is ready to bloom.”*

A whimsical, early-internet storybook garden built for the **haX.baby “Baby's First Website” Hackathon (Track 2: Original Character)**.

---

## 📖 About Morrow
- **Name:** Morrow
- **What they are:** A tiny, soft, leaf-eared garden creature with expressive eyes, a seed satchel, and dirt on their little feet. Curious, gentle, and a little forgetful.
- **What they do:** Collect forgotten ideas and nurture them into plants, inventions, and stories.
- **Their story:** Morrow appeared when a forgotten thought fell out of the internet in 2003 and landed in an empty patch of digital soil. They started growing a garden for every idea that deserved another chance.
- **Tagline:** “Not every little idea is ready to bloom.”

---

## ✨ Features & Interactive Living Works
1. **Interactive Hero Creature:** Hand-drawn SVG Morrow whose expressive eyes track the pointer on desktop and respond to touch on mobile.
2. **The Idea Greenhouse:** Terrarium with interactive mist spray and warming sunbeam lamp.
3. **The Story Orchard:** Gnarled fruit tree bearing narrative lanterns that shake down forgotten story excerpts into Morrow's harvesting basket.
4. **The Tomorrow Machine:** Steampunk botanical contraption with spinning brass cogs and a sproutometer ticker tape prognosis.
5. **Scroll-Revealed Origin Tale:** 4-scene illustrated storybook progression detailing the falling 2003 thought to Morrow's awakening.
6. **Living Interactive Garden Bed:** Plant pre-curated lost ideas or type your own custom thought; choose your seed variety (Bellflower, Fern, Star Spore, Sun Vine), and watch them bloom into clickable tags persisted in `localStorage`.
7. **Secret Easter Egg:** Find the hidden golden seed resting by Morrow's roots to wake Morrow, trigger spore/heart particle bursts, play a sparkle chime, and receive the honorary Internet Seedkeeper Certificate.
8. **Procedural Web Audio API Engine:** Authentic 2003 music-box chimes, sprout pops, and gear ticks generated mathematically without external audio files. Defaults to muted.
9. **Day / Night Theme Toggle:** Seamlessly shifts between a midnight blue starfield and warm cream sunny nursery.
10. **2003 Nostalgia Corner:** Hit counter, guestbook note, and 88x31 retro buttons.

This project has **zero external runtime dependencies**.

```bash

# or
node serve.js
```

Open [http://127.0.0.1:3000](http://127.0.0.1:3000) in your browser.
