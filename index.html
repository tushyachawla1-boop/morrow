~ Illustrated Tale ~

The Origin of Morrow

Scroll down through the chronology of how a lost byte in 2003 sprouted into a living sanctuary.

I

Scene 1: The Falling Thought

In the late autumn of 2003, a visitor typed a thought onto an internet forum and closed their browser window without pressing ‘Submit’. The unposted sentence drifted off the screen and tumbled into the deep digital void.

II

Scene 2: The Digital Loam

It didn’t vanish. It tumbled down through decommissioned webrings, forgotten IRC channels, and old server racks until it landed in a quiet patch of unallocated memory. Over the years, forgotten bookmarks and abandoned drafts had decayed into rich, fertile digital soil.

III

Scene 3: Morrow Awakens

From the warm warmth of that digital loam, two soft leaf-ears poked through the moss. Morrow opened two round, curious eyes, wiped the dirt from their tiny feet, and looked around at the vast quiet of the forgotten web.

IV

Scene 4: The First Sprout

Morrow found an old silver teaspoon to use as a trowel and planted that first fallen sentence. Days later, a luminous bellflower unfurled. Since then, Morrow has tended every idea that never had the chance to bloom.
